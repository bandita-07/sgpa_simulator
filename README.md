Project Details: SGPA_SIMULATOR PROJECT.
 
This website allows students to input their past performance, simulate current and future semester grades , track their scholarship eligibility in real-time and calculate exactly what SGPA they need to hit their target academic goals.

   To ensure data privacy - the application begins with a clean Login and registration page. Everyone creates a unique account , so that they can have a secure environment.

  The dashboard is divided into two logical sections . 
First one allows students to list their current semester subjects, credits and expected grades .
And the second one is - What-If lab where they can add future semesters or upcoming papers with targeted grades.

E.g. - If a student inputs a target CGPA like an 8.0 , the system reverses the math and prints a clear recommendation : to reach your goal , you need a What-If SGPA of 7.8 in your upcoming credits.
